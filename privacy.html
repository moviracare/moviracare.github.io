This policy explains how Movira Care ("we", "us") collects and uses personal data when you contact us or send an enquiry, including through forms on Facebook and Instagram, by phone or on WhatsApp. We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India).

2. What we collect

When you send an enquiry, we collect only what you choose to give us: your name, phone number, the area you live in, the service you need, when you need it, and any message you add. We do not ask for medical records, ID documents or payment details through enquiry forms.

3. Why we use it

  • To call or WhatsApp you about your enquiry, and to explain our services and prices.
  • To arrange the service you ask for, if you decide to go ahead.
  • To keep a simple record of enquiries so we can follow up and improve our service.

We use your data on the basis of your consent, which you give when you submit the form. We do not sell your data and do not share it with other businesses for their marketing.

4. Who can see it

Only Movira Care staff who handle enquiries. When you use a Facebook or Instagram form, Meta Platforms processes the data under its own privacy policy. We store enquiry records in password-protected business accounts.

5. How long we keep it

If you do not become a client, we delete your enquiry data within 12 months, or sooner if you ask. Client records are kept for as long as the law requires.

6. Your rights

You may ask us to show, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, at any time. Contact our grievance officer at moviracare@gmail.com or +91 70512 29555. We will reply within 7 working days. You may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
